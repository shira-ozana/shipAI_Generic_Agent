Existing guidance files (AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, .cursor/rules, README, docs) are
human-authored. Link to them. Do not copy them in full and do not contradict them.
When the evidence shows two instructions that conflict, list the conflict in
`conflicts` with both paths. Do not decide it.
